Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Policy version 2026-11-04

1. About the service

G.O.C Academy Hub is an educational platform operated by G.O.C Academy for students preparing for academic study, especially JAMB/UTME-focused learning. The app provides lessons, revision practice, quizzes, mock CBT exams, progress tracking and academy updates.

2. Terms of use

By creating an account and using the Hub, you agree to use the service only for lawful educational purposes. You must not:

  • Use the platform for cheating, impersonation, unauthorised sharing or exam misconduct.
  • Attempt to access or alter another student's data, admin systems or course content.
  • Upload harmful code, malware or content designed to disrupt the service.
  • Use the app to harass, threaten or abuse any student, coach or staff member.

We may suspend or deactivate accounts that violate these conditions or that compromise the safety and integrity of the learning environment.

3. Account and student information

Students are responsible for keeping their login details secure and for using only their own personal account. Each account is intended for the individual student and should not be shared with another person. Progress records, subject choices and study history are personal academic data.

4. Privacy policy

Information we collect: We may collect name, email address, phone number, password data, examination goal, subject combination, study progress, scores, quiz attempts, lesson completion data and support messages.

How we use it: This information is used to provide account access, personalise learning paths, track progress, improve academic support, verify eligibility for enrolled programmes and communicate important updates, reminders and support responses. These are the educational and operational purposes of the platform.

Publicity: The academy may use aggregated, anonymised performance statistics (for example, overall pass rates or average scores) to describe its programmes publicly. Anything that identifies a student personally — a name, photograph, result or testimonial — is only published with that student's (or their guardian's) separate, explicit permission, which can be withdrawn by contacting the academy.

Your consent record: Registration requires you to tick the acceptance box below the sign-up form. When you do, we store the date and time of your acceptance together with the policy version you accepted, against your Scholar ID, so we can show what you agreed to.

How we protect it: We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including secure access controls, encrypted transmission where available, role-based account restrictions and limited access to data. No system is completely risk-free, so we ask users to protect their own login information and report any suspicious access immediately.

Data storage: Some information may be stored in local browser storage, server-side app data or third-party hosting and database services used to run the platform. We keep data only for as long as needed to provide the service or to satisfy legal, academic or support requirements.

5. Cookies, local storage and site analytics

The app uses browser storage (not advertising cookies) to remember your session, preferences, study progress and local settings. This helps the platform work reliably and remember your recent activity. You can clear local browser data at any time, but doing so may log you out or reset some saved preferences.

The platform includes a simple visit counter that records which screens were opened, how long a visit lasted, and whether the device is a phone or a computer. The server stores only a daily-rotating hash of the IP address — never the raw address — and no name, email or Scholar ID travels with it. This counter runs only after the privacy policy has been accepted on the device (at registration, or when a registered student signs in); visits by people who have not accepted the policy are not recorded. There is no third-party advertising tracker.

6. Third-party services

We may rely on hosting providers, messaging tools, email delivery services and analytics or support infrastructure to operate the platform. These providers are used only to support the service and are expected to handle data lawfully and responsibly under their own privacy obligations.

7. Student support and contact

If you have questions about this policy, your account information, academic records or a data request, you can contact the academy by email at godsowncenterinfo@gmail.com or by phone at 0815 721 5942.

8. Changes to the policy

We may update these terms and this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, academic operations or applicable law. When updates are made, the revised policy will be published in the platform and the effective date will be updated.

9. Disclaimer

The content and study materials in the app are for educational support and are not a guarantee of exam success, results or admission. Students remain responsible for their own learning, preparation and academic decisions.
