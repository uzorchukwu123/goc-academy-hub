By creating an account and using the Hub, you agree to use the service only for lawful educational purposes. You must not:

  • Use the platform for cheating, impersonation, unauthorised sharing or exam misconduct.

  • Use the app to harass, threaten or abuse any student, coach or staff member.

We may suspend or deactivate accounts that violate these conditions or that compromise the safety and integrity of the learning environment.

3. Account and student information

Students are responsible for keeping their login details secure and for using only their own personal account. Each account is intended for the individual student and should not be shared with another person. Progress records, subject choices and study history are personal academic data.

4. Privacy policy

Information we collect: We may collect name, email address, phone number, password data, examination goal, subject combination, study progress, scores, quiz attempts, lesson completion data and support messages.

How we use it: This information is used to provide account access, personalise learning paths, track progress, improve academic support, verify eligibility for enrolled programmes and communicate important updates, reminders and support responses.

How we protect it: We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including secure access controls, encrypted transmission where available, role-based account restrictions and limited access to data. No system is completely risk-free, so we ask users to protect their own login information and report any suspicious access immediately.

Data storage: Some information may be stored in local browser storage, server-side app data or third-party hosting and database services used to run the platform. We keep data only for as long as needed to provide the service or to satisfy legal, academic or support requirements.

5. Cookies and local storage

6. Third-party services
