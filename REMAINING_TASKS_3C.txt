   - The way in: type the secret word into the Scholar ID box on the Log in screen and press Log in.

     page sends what was typed to POST /api/door and the server compares it.

BEFORE YOU USE IT
   - Set GOC_TUTOR_DOOR (start-server.ps1 has a commented line; DEPLOYMENT.md lists it). Without it
     tutors cannot reach their sign-in. Give tutors the word in person or by private message.
   - Hidden is not secure: anyone who reads the page code can see that a word is sent to the server,
     but not what it is. What protects the data is the tutor sign-in and the scope guard.

   - data.json now holds tutor password hashes: it is already on the "keep out of public deploys"
     list, and server/backups/ copies hold them too.
